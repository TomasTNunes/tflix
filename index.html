Windows
No Ads
Download
  1. Download the installer and open it.
  2. If your browser says the file isn't commonly downloaded, choose Keep. If Windows shows Windows protected your PC, click More info → Run anyway.
  3. Click through the setup (Next → Install), then open TFLIX from the desktop or Start menu.
Updating

If a new version is out, a notice appears when you open the app and your profile icon gets a yellow dot. Click Update here and run the new installer over the old version. There's no need to uninstall, and your settings are kept.

Android & Android TV
No Ads
Download
Phone or tablet
  1. Download the APK, then open it from the download notification or your Downloads folder.
  2. When asked, allow Install unknown apps for your browser or Files app.
  3. Tap Install, then open TFLIX.
Android TV
  1. Install a file manager from the Play Store, such as Send files to TV or X-plore.
  2. Get the APK onto the TV with it (or download it on the TV), open it and allow unknown apps when asked.
  3. TFLIX then shows up in the TV's app list.
Updating

If a new version is out, a notice appears when you open the app and your profile icon gets a yellow dot. Tap Update here and the new APK downloads, no browser needed. On a phone or tablet, tap the finished download to install it over the old version. On a TV the installer opens by itself. The first time, Android asks you to allow installs from that source (your Downloads or Files app on a phone, TFLIX on a TV): allow it, come back and tap Update again. There's no need to uninstall.

Already have the app? Install the new version over it to update. There's no need to uninstall.
