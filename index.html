Android & Android TV
No Ads
Download
Phone or tablet
  1. Download the APK, then open it from the download notification or your Downloads folder.
  2. When asked, allow Install unknown apps for your browser or Files app.
  3. If Google Play Protect warns about or blocks the app, tap More details → Install anyway.
  4. Tap Install, then open TFLIX.
Android TV
  1. Install a file manager or file-sharing app from the Play Store, such as Send files to TV, X-plore or LocalSend.
  2. Get the APK onto the TV with it (or download it on the TV), open it and allow unknown apps when asked.
  3. If Google Play Protect warns about or blocks the app, select More details (or More info) → Install anyway.
  4. TFLIX then shows up in the TV's app list.
Updating

If a new version is out, a notice appears when you open the app and your profile icon gets a yellow dot. Tap Update here and the new APK downloads, no browser needed. On a phone or tablet, tap the finished download to install it over the old version. On a TV the installer opens by itself. The first time, Android asks you to allow installs from that source (your Downloads or Files app on a phone, TFLIX on a TV): allow it, come back and tap Update again.
If Google Play Protect blocks the update, choose More details → Install anyway. There's no need to uninstall.

Windows
No Ads
Download
  1. Download the installer and open it.
  2. If your browser says the file isn't commonly downloaded, choose Keep. If Windows shows Windows protected your PC, click More info → Run anyway.
  3. Click through the setup (Next → Install), then open TFLIX from the desktop or Start menu.
Updating

If a new version is out, a notice appears when you open the app and your profile icon gets a yellow dot. Click Update here and run the new installer over the old version. There's no need to uninstall, and your settings are kept.

Linux
No Ads
Download

Download lists the Linux files: a .deb or an AppImage, for Intel/AMD (x64) (most computers) or ARM64 (a Raspberry Pi or an ARM laptop).

.deb (Ubuntu, Debian, Mint, Raspberry Pi OS)
  1. Download the .deb and open it, or run sudo apt install ./tflix-linux.*.deb in the folder you saved it to.
  2. Open TFLIX from your app menu.
AppImage (other distros)
  1. Download the AppImage, then right-click it → Properties → Allow executing as program (or run chmod +x on it).
  2. Double-click it to open TFLIX. Nothing gets installed.
  3. On Ubuntu 24.04 and later, the first time you open it, click Allow and open TFLIX and enter your password (or use the .deb instead).
Updating

If a new version is out, a notice appears when you open the app and your profile icon gets a yellow dot. Click Update here: the Linux files open with yours (the same kind, for the same processor) highlighted, so click its Update. Open the new .deb to install it over the old version, or swap the old AppImage for the new one. There's no need to uninstall, and your settings are kept.

Add TFLIX
iPhone and iPad
  1. Open tflix.stream in Safari.
  2. Tap Share . On iOS 26, tap ••• first. It's at the bottom on iPhone and at the top on iPad.
  3. Tap Add to Home Screen. If you don't see it, scroll down or tap View More.
  4. If you see Open as Web App, make sure it's on. Then tap Add, and TFLIX shows up on your Home Screen.
Mac
  1. Open tflix.stream in Safari (macOS Sonoma or later).
  2. Choose File → Add to Dock, then click Add.
Block the adsRecommended
iPhone and iPad: AdGuard DNS

Free, and it blocks ads, pop-ups and redirects on the whole device, not only in TFLIX. It won't catch every single ad, but it stops most of them, so watching is much more pleasant. You can turn it off anytime in Settings.

Download AdGuard DNS
  1. Tap Download AdGuard DNS in Safari, then Allow.
  2. Open the Settings app.
  3. Tap Profile Downloaded, near the top.
  4. Tap Install and follow the steps on screen.
  5. If it isn't on by itself, go to Settings → General → VPN & Device Management → DNS and choose AdGuard DNS.
Screenshots: AdGuard
Mac: an ad blocker for Safari

Get one from the Mac App Store, such as AdGuard for Safari or uBlock Origin Lite, and turn it on in Safari → Settings → Extensions. On macOS Sequoia or later it also works in TFLIX in the Dock (check TFLIX → Settings → Extensions).

AdGuard DNS works on a Mac too, but it filters every app on the computer. Set it up on AdGuard's site.

Good to know

TFLIX on the Home Screen or Dock keeps its own data, apart from Safari. Sign in, or create a free account, to keep My List and Continue Watching in sync. There's nothing to update: it always opens the latest TFLIX.
